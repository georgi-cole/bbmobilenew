**Description**

Enter The Big Eye, a single-player social strategy game where every conversation, competition, nomination, and vote can reshape the season.

Create your player, meet a cast of distinctive housemates, and decide how you want to play. Build trust, form alliances, manage rivalries, and read the room before each ceremony. When power is on the line, compete in a varied collection of interactive minigames. When the vote arrives, your social game matters just as much as your wins.

FEATURES

- Play a complete, evolving competition season.
- Develop alliances, friendships, and rivalries.
- Compete in skill, memory, logic, timing, and risk-based minigames.
- Make nomination, safety, and eviction decisions.
- Experience twists, dramatic ceremonies, recaps, and a full finale.
- Review your season history and memorable moments.
- Play the core season without creating an account.

Every season creates a different story. Will you control the house, survive from the bottom, or win when it matters most?
